How it works

From questions to chaos in three steps.

Setting up takes minutes. Nobody needs an account, and the host's laptop runs the whole game.

01

Write it, or drop in a doc

Type questions in the editor, or import a Word document, PDF or text file. Oya finds the questions, the options and the right answer, then lets you check before you play.

02

Share the PIN

Open the game on a laptop and put it on a projector or TV. Players scan the QR code or type the link, enter the 6-digit PIN and make their avatar.

03

Play it live

Questions appear on the big screen, answers on every phone. Points for speed, bonus for streaks, then a scoreboard and a podium at the end.

The good stuff

Everything that makes a room shout.

Make your look

Before they join, players build an avatar: gele or afro, cap or locs, shades, skin tone, colour. Then their face pops up on the big screen.

Streaks and an animated scoreboard

Fast answers score more. Get a few right in a row and the streak bonus kicks in. After each question, the scoreboard slides into its new order.
