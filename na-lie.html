How it works

Four steps. Zero preparation.

Open Na Lie! on a laptop connected to a screen, share the PIN, and the game runs itself. A round per player, until everyone has had their turn.

Scoring

Two ways to win.

Sharp eyes score points every round. A good poker face scores on your own turn. The best players do both.

+1,000Spot the lie500 points for picking the lie, plus up to 500 more for answering fast.
+150Fool the roomOn your turn in the hot seat, you earn 150 points for every person who falls for one of your truths.

Made for rooms

Best with people who think they know each other.

New classes and cohorts

Day one of a bootcamp or a new term. Everyone learns names and stories in fifteen minutes.

Team bonding

Remote or in the office, it gets people talking about more than work.

Youth groups and fellowships

A lively warm-up before the main event, with the whole room involved.

Parties and owambes

Friends who think they know each other. They're about to find out.

The host is in control

Set it up in one short screen before the lobby opens, and steer the game while it runs.

  • Hot-seat turnsEveryone, or a set number (5 to 15) for big groups. Everyone still votes every round.
  • Writing and voting time60 seconds to 2 minutes to write; 15 to 30 seconds to vote.
  • Skip a playerIf a statement is too personal or not appropriate, skip it before it reaches the screen.
  • End any timeJump straight to the podium, confetti and share cards whenever you like.
